Agentic Carrier Submissions Pipeline

The same multi-carrier workflow, before and after automation.

Princeton PO submissions automation is an agentic Claude Code system I designed and shipped to automate how the organization reports provider changes to its 11-plus contracted insurance carriers. Built canonical-first, it extracts each source document once into one data model, then format-specific writers generate every carrier deliverable (the Standard and Horizon submissions, the SugarCRM import, and the Letters of Intent) through direct CRM and address-validation APIs, backed by a 1,000-plus-test regression suite, an audit harness, a self-improving learning loop, and human QA on every batch. The result: a 70% reduction in hands-on preparation time per submission, and carrier submission rework reduced from 15% to under 2%.
